Supplier Credit — Easy Rules

Simple guide for unpaid pharmacy purchases against suppliers.

What is Credit Limit?

Maximum unpaid amount you can owe this supplier. Example: limit PKR 50,000 means unpaid purchase bills cannot go above 50,000.

Credit Limit = 0

Credit purchase not allowed. You must pay the purchase in full (cash/card/bank). Unpaid portion is blocked.

Opening Balance

Old unpaid amount already owed to the supplier. It counts toward used credit together with new unpaid purchases.

How purchases are blocked

When you create a purchase with unpaid amount, the system blocks it if outstanding + this unpaid bill exceeds the supplier credit limit.
